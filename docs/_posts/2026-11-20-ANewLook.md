Our event pages have a new look (potentially). Jeff Taylor has built a new look for [Day of Data Jacksonville 2027](https://dayofdata.org/2027-05-15-dayofdata1168/). Check it out and send your feedback. 

Each organizer can decide how their event looks, and over the years I've added various capabilities and customizations for different events, but Jeff took this to a new level. I'm excited to see what others think and if they want to use the layout. Here's a small look at the site:

![Jacksonville's New Look](/assets/img/blog/jax_newlook.png)

For comparison, here is last year's site:

![Jacksonville's Old Site](/assets/img/blog/jax_oldlook.png)

Jeff has experimented with some new tools and ways of running events. I'm looking forward to a blog post soon on how this works. I'm excited to see if this makes a difference in the events.

I'm also hoping I can find some time, or get others, to experiment and add new tools and ideas that can help others. Running these events takes a lot of effort. Any tools that make things easier are welcome.

We do maintain a public repo of tools, which is rather sparse, but feel free to fork and PR new tools if you have ideas here: [SQLSat Tools](https://github.com/sqlsaturday/sqlsattools)
