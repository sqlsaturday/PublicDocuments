Each year I contact [Redgate Software](https://www.red-gate.com/) to discuss their support for the annual PASS Data Community Summit. Redgate has been a long-time supporter of the event, and this year they have generously provided me with a number of free tickets to give away to event organizers.

At the start of the year, I reached out and after some internal discussions, I received a positive message that once again, Redgate would provide me with free tickets to give away to event organizers for the 2026 PASS Data Community Summit.

If you're interested in organizing an event, it's not hard. Contact us and we'll point you in the right direction. We need more events, and we need more organizers. If you want to get involved, please reach out.

## Reminders

[SQL Saturday is an independent US 501.c.3 corporation](https://blog.sqlsaturday.com/2021-11-18-nonprofit/) organized as a public charity. We are not owned by, or managed by Redgate Software. Redgate donated the brand to the corporation and gave us a grant to run events, but there is no oversight of the corporation. Redgate does maintain a permanent seat on the board of directors, currently occupied by Steve Jones, but has no more influence than any of the other members of the board.

If you or others want to join our mailing list, you can register here: [http://eepurl.com/hwVBKn](http://eepurl.com/hwVBKn). Please feel free to pass this along to others.
